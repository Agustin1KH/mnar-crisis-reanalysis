# Phase-2 falsification trajectory: |Δ INCOME| across shadows

Generated by hand from `output/tables/phase2_shadow_<sv>/03a_exclusion_diagnostic.md`
across the three shadows. Companion to `phase2_exclusion_across_shadows.md`
and `phase2_gap_sum_estimators_across_shadows.md`. Reports the
falsification-refit deltas for the active shadow on `gap_sum` Spec A.

The 03a falsification refits are designed to test whether the Heckman
identification is leaning on the shadow variable parametrically. Two
variants per shadow:

- **Falsif 1**: drop the active shadow from the selection equation,
  keeping `maddison_priority` as the remaining excluded covariate.
  Resulting selection eq: `r ~ candidate + year + maddison_priority`.
- **Falsif 2**: drop *both* the active shadow and `maddison_priority`,
  leaving only `r ~ candidate + year`. Year is then in both equations
  so identification is purely parametric (bivariate-normal functional
  form alone). Strict reading.

Threshold for "concerning": `|Δ INCOME| > 0.03` OR sign flip on rho
between original and falsified fit.

## Reading

The memo's current stability argument cites Falsif 1 |Δ INCOME| = 0.0029
under `n_chron_clean`. Under `n_chron_3chron_raw` the same falsification
gives 0.0096 — still well below the 0.03 threshold, but **3.3× larger**
than the n_chron_clean baseline. Falsif 2 (the strict reading) climbs
from 0.0146 → 0.0270, a 1.8× increase that lands within striking
distance of the 0.03 threshold.

This is consistent with the broader Phase-2 finding: stripping LV makes
the Heckman correction *do more work* on `gap_sum`. Stronger selection
signal (IMR \|t\|: 0.58 → 2.02; ρ \|t\|: 0.51 → 1.98; GJRM τ: −0.082 →
−0.224) translates mechanically into larger sensitivity of the corrected
INCOME estimate to the choice of identifying restriction. The original
"0.0029, an order of magnitude below 0.03" framing weakens to "0.0096
on Falsif 1 / 0.0270 on Falsif 2 — under threshold but not by an order
of magnitude" under the cleaner shadow.

For the memo: this is honest material for the Section 2.3 rewrite (the
shadow-variable-credibility paragraph). The 03a falsification was always
the strongest single-test for shadow-variable validity; under the
cleaner shadow it goes from "decisively negligible" to "still passing,
but with notably less margin." Neither Falsif 1 nor Falsif 2 crosses
the 0.03 threshold under any of the three shadows; the rho sign
remains negative throughout (no flip). The substantive conclusion that
the continuous-outcome INCOME does not depend on the shadow for
identification still holds — just less emphatically.
